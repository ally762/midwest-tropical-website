Custom Water Feature Manufacturer

Welcome to Midwest Tropical, the most experienced designer and fabricator of custom water features. For almost fifty years, we’ve been acknowledged for pioneering and perfecting a variety of innovative, award-winning water feature designs, including Bubble Walls, Indoor Waterfalls, Rain Curtains, Water Walls, and Reflection Pools.

Midwest Tropical is known as the premier manufacturer of high quality, low-maintenance, water features and offers nationwide installation. We have built many large-scale water feature projects around the world, including a Bubble Wall spanning 50 feet wide for a children’s hospital in Michigan.

Since 1977, we’ve been building custom water features in our state-of-the-art facility in Skokie, Illinois and are proud to have our water features “Made in America” by trained craftsmen with decades of experience.  We have invented and innovated some of the most popular water feature designs including the patented Bubble Wall, invented by our founder, and maintenance-free Enclosed Water Walls!

Our name may be Midwest Tropical, but we’ve installed stunning features in every state of the U.S.A. and around the world.  In addition to building the finest water features, we offer turn-key nationwide installations for our custom water features.

Bubble Walls

Midwest Tropical invented the Bubble Wall which combines Water, Air and Light into a stunning water effect with bubbles slowly ascending in Sealed & Enclosed Water Feature Design. Color changing LED lighting allows you to customize the color to fit any environment and with larger Bubble Wall Projects we offer the capability to utilize computer controlled DMX lighting and air controls to create stunning “Shows”; think of it as the Bellagio Fountain of Indoor Water Features!
Explore Bubble Walls

Rain Curtain

A Rain Curtain is a type of water feature that gives the illusion of a wall or curtain of rain, creating a visually stunning and relaxing environment. Water is recirculates from the basin to the top of the feature and then falls along invisible strands to create an enthralling curtain effect with no splash!
Explore Rain Curtains

Glass Water Walls

Glass Water Walls offer a timeless water feature design with water cascading down a glass surface. With many different glass options available including frosted, textured, specialty and pattern glasses to give your water wall a unique look. Midwest Tropical offers both traditional open Water Walls where you can touch the water, as well Sealed and Enclosed Water Walls where all the water is encapsulated between glass panels for a no-maintenance design. Etched, colored and even raised dimensional logos can be added to our glass water walls to help brand your space!

Scored Waterfalls

Midwest Tropical uses a proprietary textured material to create its Scored Waterfalls. The water slowly descends down the specially designed surface to create a rippled wave pattern as the water cascades from top to bottom. Logos and lighting can be added to these Scored Waterfalls to make customized for your space.
Explore Scored Waterfalls

Mesh Water Walls

As calming as it is functional, a mesh water feature creates an impressive and eye catching focal point for any space indoors and outdoors. Mesh Waterfalls offer a beautiful, functional solution that can be customized to any size, offer color changing lighting, and create a stunning cascading water pattern as the water slowly descends down this proprietary metal specially designed by Midwest Tropical.
Explore Mesh Water Walls

Confidence Sealed into Every Midwest Water Feature

Get peace of mind with every custom feature we craft from Midwest tropical water fountains, Midwest Tropical Waterfalls and Waterwalls. Put your concerns about leakage away. Sealing displays upright and tight is the number one focus at Midwest Water Features, and over the many years we’ve been in business, we’ve innovated and improved the physical beauty, functionality, and structural integrity of our products. Count on the trust earned through Midwest’s dedication to exceptional products and pleased clients worldwide.

WATERPROOFING. Effective waterproofing is achieved through Midwest’s top-of-the-line liners, sealants, or membranes specifically designed for water containment.

MATERIALS WITH NO COMPROMISE. Count on our selection of high-performance, long-lasting materials such as pipes, fittings, and connectors. They must be durable, corrosion-resistant, and specifically designed for water applications.

PROPER SEALING AND JOINTS. We ensure all joints, connections, and seams perform to expectations and beyond. This includes using waterproofing sealants, gaskets, or rubber liners to seal any potential gaps or areas prone to leakage.

ADEQUATE DRAINAGE AND OVERFLOW. Proper drainage and overflow systems are essential. Our proprietary systems allow excess water to be safely directed away from the feature, reducing the risk of leaks due to increased water volume.

REGULAR MAINTENANCE AND INSPECTIONS. Routine maintenance and inspections are crucial to identify and address any potential issues before they develop into leaks or safety hazards.
